Solar Ray & Wind Ventilation Impact on HDB Flats

How Open-Meteo atmospheric metrics directly correlate with your housing choices

🌬️ Wind Speed at 10m (~3rd to 4th Floor Level):

Wind speed measured at 10 meters indicates natural cross-ventilation potential. Units on mid-to-high floors (6th floor and above) typically experience 20–40% higher airflow, enabling effective passive cooling without relying solely on air conditioning.

☀️ Temperature & Solar Azimuth Heating:

During peak ambient temperatures (1:00 PM – 4:30 PM), West and North-West facing facades absorb significant solar thermal energy. Units with North-South orientations stay cooler because the sun does not strike living room window glass directly.
